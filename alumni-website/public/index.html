Message from the Chancellor

It is a great opportunity for me to introduce the best university of central India under the umbrella of Vanshpati Smriti Siksha Samiti (VSSS) Bhopal - a dedicated Society for promoting higher education. We believe in an education system that looks beyond employment and concentrates on individual development by imparting skills and inculcating human values. Academic excellence, holistic education, innovative approach and global relevance happen to be the corner stone of VSSS which is emulated in its essence by Madhyanchal Professional University.

What was a small start in the year 1975 has grown to a sizable population of about 11K members now and we are at a milestone year of Golden Jubilee. Hope to see a vibrant Golden Jubilee Year with the involvement of all the esteemed alumni worldwide.

Mrs. Preeti Patel
Chancellor, MPU Alumni Association
